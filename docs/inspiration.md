## Adaptation

The shared idea is indirect control: autonomous units follow simple rules while the player changes the route. Neon Convoy gives that idea an industrial science-fiction identity: wheeled drones, lasers, drills, explosive projectiles, construction, traffic control, and landing jets.

Early levels introduce one operation at a time. Later ones combine routing below steel, safe descents, destruction, and construction under time and tool constraints. Modern conveniences include pause-time assignment, fast forward, per-level hints, freely selectable sectors, persistent records, and an unranked terrain editor.

All game visuals are drawn with code. The renderer creates an original multi-state sprite sheet and animated industrial scene. The ten soundtracks are original procedural Web Audio sequences; no recorded commercial music is used. Google Fonts are optional typographic resources loaded by the stylesheet.
